# Composition of isometries of Euclidean plane

## Outline

This program simulates compositions of basic isometries of Euclidean plane, that is:

- The composition of two reflections with parallel lines is a parallel translation.
- The composition of two reflections is a rotation.
- The composition of two rotations of different centers is a rotation of some center.
- The composition of two same glide reflections is a parallel translation.
